# ContainerHub v3 图像生成记录

日期：2026-10-22。工具：内置 image_gen（非 CLI）。最终资产为生成的 PNG；没有将栅格图冒充矢量原稿。

## 初始品牌图形

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: the primary standalone brand symbol for ContainerHub, a polished native macOS container management application built with Rust and GPUI. Product concept: multiple isolated containers brought together into a single controllable hub.
Create ONE carefully designed, distinctive pictorial brand mark, not a monogram and not a wordmark. Develop the concept of a compact docking cluster: three solid modular container forms interlock around a precisely cut central negative-space opening. The arrangement should suggest one cohesive architectural object, with a clear strong silhouette and a memorable deliberate open notch. Subtle axonometric geometry is welcome, but the result must read as a graphic identity, not a 3D illustration or a diagram. The individual modules should feel related and meticulously proportioned. Use generous clean negative space and optical alignment. It should be recognisable at 24px.
Style: senior identity designer, restrained contemporary infrastructure software brand. Bold filled shapes, precise edges, slightly softened corners only where useful. Two flat blue tones: cobalt #2463EB and deep blue #1746A2, optionally a small lighter blue face #5792F5 for structural clarity. No other colors.
Composition: one centered symbol, almost square silhouette, occupy approximately 76% of a 1024x1024 canvas, generous transparent space around it. Genuine transparent background, no tile or circle behind the mark. No text, no letters C or H, no caption, no mockup, no design presentation board, no variants.
Avoid: ordinary outlined cube icon, shipping-box clipart, Docker whale, Kubernetes wheel, generic network dots, hexagon badge, infinity symbol, AI sparkle, green or purple, gradients, glow, shadows, reflections, thin strokes, glass, metallic material, extraneous detail. Deliver a crisp, deliberate, vector-like brand drawing with true alpha transparency.
```

## 品牌图形精修

```text
Use case: logo-brand refinement.
Input image: the provided blue three-module ContainerHub brand symbol is the EDIT TARGET. Preserve this exact identity, overall silhouette, arrangement of the three modules, central negative-space hub, perspective, and generous transparent margin.
Refine ONLY the execution into a clean professional identity master: perfectly smooth geometric contours, consistently rounded corners, straight precise internal edges, no stray blue fragments or colored fringe in the empty channels, especially the narrow vertical gap between the two bottom modules. Make all three separating channels equally deliberate and optically balanced. Replace the subtle grain and lighting gradients with truly flat solid fills, cobalt #2463EB on the upper/main faces and deep blue #1746A2 on the side faces; no texture and no shaded render.
ONE symbol on genuinely transparent background. No text, no border, no surrounding tile, no shadow, no presentation board, no additional variants. Crisp antialiased edges; the central cutout and all separating gaps must also have true transparency. This is the final small-size software brand asset, not a concept illustration.
```

## 最终应用图标

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: NEW opaque square application icon asset based on the provided ContainerHub symbol as an identity reference, not a transparent cutout edit.
Create a separate application icon: full-bleed solid cobalt-blue square background (#2463EB), extending exactly to all four canvas edges. No rounded outer corners in the image itself: the platform will mask the app icon. No transparency.
At the center, faithfully redraw the reference's three interlocking modular container forms and central negative-space hub, in clean white primary faces with pale icy-blue side faces. Keep the same recognizable three-part docking-cluster silhouette. The symbol should occupy about 67% of the square width. Leave substantial even padding. Make the central hub and the three separating channels clean cobalt background. Optically balance the spacing. No letters.
Finish as a carefully drawn flat graphic application icon: mathematically crisp smooth contours, flat solid colors, consistent small corner radii in the symbol, clean antialiasing, no texture, no grain, no stray pixels. It must work as a premium native infrastructure tool icon at 32px. Use the same symbol, not a new idea.
Output one 1024x1024 square icon only. No mockup, no caption, no drop shadow, no rim, no outer border, no floating tile, no outer padding beyond the blue square, no gradient or gloss.
```
